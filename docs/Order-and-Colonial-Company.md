# Order, Colonial Company, and Central Relationship

[Project index](../README.md) · [Project status](Project-Status.md) · [Creative vision](Creative-Vision.md)

Developed November 1, 2026; updated November 5, 2026. Status labels below distinguish the user's selected character direction from institutional proposals and historical references. Character names, exact fictional dates, and full biographies are not established.

## Confirmed relationship direction

The protagonist and the recurring colleague belong to the same institution and have met before, but do not know one another well. A single brief previous meeting is a possible detail, not a fixed event. Their starting connection rests on shared membership and professional recognition, leaving personal trust and affection room to develop through the assignments.

The protagonist is newer to the institution. This does not make them an apprentice: members should be capable of, and expected to perform, independent work.

The colleague is a more experienced operator, previously sent to the colonies because of their expertise. The user used “veteran” provisionally to express relative experience; advanced age, a long service history, and formal senior rank are not implied.

The colleague is not the protagonist's designated mentor. They can provide practical advice, local orientation, professional support, and occasional guidance without making the protagonist dependent on instruction or constant accompaniment. The colleague's specialty, temperament, authority, and personal history remain Open.

## Confirmed assignment intake

Approved November 4, 2026.

Most assignments begin with a client who believes they have convincing evidence of supernatural involvement. The company receives both requests to investigate unexplained trouble and requests to resolve an apparently recognized threat; the two overlap.

The company must establish what is happening before deciding what intervention is appropriate. Keep distinct what the client experienced, their explanation, and whom or what they hold responsible. A client can correctly recognize supernatural involvement while misunderstanding its cause, intentions or connection to another person.

Treat clients as people bringing serious concerns and their best explanations. Fear, prior experience, local teachings and relationships can affect their judgments. This default allows straightforward jobs, sincere mistakes, concealed information and unfamiliar phenomena without making every client foolish or deceptive. Company members' own interpretations can also be wrong.

This describes the usual starting point, not a required plot formula or a guarantee of supernatural involvement. It does not establish universal household knowledge, particular beings or effective precautions. Pricing, access, legal authority and specific investigative methods remain Open.

## Confirmed institutional origin and development

Approved November 4, 2026 as a broad fictional outline; supplemented November 5 with the user's selection of the historical Order of St John as the institutional basis. Its supernatural branch and occult activities are original fiction, not historical findings.

The older parent institution's public-service roots and the fictional specialists' organizational history must now be distinguished. The selected parent is the Order of St John, with its historical hospital and religious-military background. The prior decision that mutual support becomes central to the specialists' organization remains in force; its development and relationship to the parent order remain Open.

The fictional specialists use occult methods. That use gives government or church authorities a reason to distance themselves publicly. The exact practices, grounds for objection, degree of distancing, and differences among authorities remain unresolved. This does not establish universal hostility, secret patronage, complete illegality or a single government/church position.

The fictional supernatural branch's original need, founder, date, sanction and conditions of support remain Open. These are distinct from the real parent order's documented religious foundation. The user favors the English suppression of 1540 as a possible occasion for forming the branch and requests a substantial supernatural reason; this is an origin proposal, not an approved event. Whether 1540 marks new knowledge, formal organization of older practice or another change remains unresolved. See [historical and folklore candidates](Institutional-Origin-Research.md).

## Institutional details — Open

The selected parent order is international and has substantial historical English connections; it did not originate in England. The branch's name, geographic reach, religious obligations, legal privileges and reputation remain Open. The earlier preference for respect at home is not evidence of uninterrupted official English standing after 1540. The extent of historical divergence and the branch's relationship to the wider order remain to be developed.

Its colonial operation is comparatively new and not yet locally established. The user proposes that it might operate or be known as a company because colonial circumstances require a different practical organization. “Order” and “company” could describe different forms of the same institution, rather than two unrelated groups.

The reason for the local organizational form, precise limits of members' established independent-working expectation, financing, membership, and contractual duties are not decided. The user has suggested that individual operators' payment arrangements could vary; no fee policy or funding model has been selected. Do not assume historically attested occult activity, a specific colonial charter or a modern business structure. The religious parent has been selected; the fictional branch's obligations and colonial legal form remain Open.

The user clarified the current research objective: find a possible basis for an induction or transformation that produces a fearsome appearance and physical abilities moderately beyond an ordinary human's. The source, rite, visible changes, risks, limits and exact abilities remain Open. Navarre as an organizational catalyst and a rogue Hospitaller investigating healing through necromancy are current proposals, not established origins; see [the research clarification](Institutional-Origin-Research.md#chronology-and-clarified-research-purpose).

Reputation must be contextual: established respect at home need not guarantee colonial familiarity or universal approval. Connections to England alone should not produce general hostility; local affiliations, conduct, and supernatural practices can matter. Historical background references include the [1629 Massachusetts charter](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/17th_century/mass03.asp) and [Colonial Williamsburg's study of Anglican Virginia](https://research.colonialwilliamsburg.org/DigitalLibrary/view/index.cfm?doc=ResearchReports%5CRR0006.xml).

## Migration as a relationship influence

The user wants to explore how England-to-America travel and separation affected immigrants' relationships, and to use that experience to inform the characters' connection.

Historical references, not fictional canon:

- [Plimoth Patuxet Museums](https://plimoth.org/for-students/homework-help/who-were-the-pilgrims) describes the Mayflower's 66-day crossing and families divided when some passengers had to remain behind. This is one voyage, not a universal crossing duration.
- [John Winthrop's 1630 letters to his wife, in a modernized transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/66701/old/66701-h/66701-h.htm) connect affection and separation with family responsibilities, news carried by ships, bereavement, and plans to reunite. Their particular relationship should not be generalized to all migrants.
- [The National Archives' Prize Papers project](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/professional-guidance-and-services/our-research-and-academic-collaboration/our-research-projects/collections-and-archives-research/the-prize-papers/) documents intercepted, undelivered correspondence from 1652–1815, offering evidence of communication's vulnerability. This date range may extend beyond the eventual setting.

Possible story implications include a familiar colleague acquiring greater significance far from home, reputations being built on incomplete or outdated reports, and practical assistance developing into affection. These are proposals to test, not universal historical psychological claims or approved events.

## Open narrative possibilities

The user raised this as a possibility; no disappearance, death, betrayal, rescue plot, or forced outcome is established. It does not revive the earlier unselected premise of arriving to search for a missing first company member.
